# VALR-T22 — Current-main V0 certification manifest

## Basis and audit rules

The production certification candidate SHA for this manifest is
`4efb1d346c926f2ee10654c3bc24cd92af351881`, the exact current-main candidate
recorded by T23 and the candidate T23 must consume. This checkout starts from
current `origin/main` `657e571ced6e06219e9d1a065775d762e4a83279`, which is an
evidence-only descendant containing the T23 ledger; this manifest and its
later docs-only descendants do not change the production candidate. The prior
`95f7e7a0233cfa917d0c9656b990fd2af4996874` T11 baseline is historical input,
not the current candidate. This is a review artifact, not a certification
decision; T25 owns the final V0 decision, and T21 owns Stage-3 README,
roadmap, and index/status reconciliation.

The ten headings below are the only top-level domain mapping. Each capability
appears once. `Validator` means an executable public-consumer scenario and its
named test. `Core/internal` means complementary implementation or contract
evidence and never substitutes for Validator evidence. `PG18` is required only
where the frozen T08 matrix says so. For those rows, the required command is
the T20 trusted gate and its workflow artifact; a row-level pass from a failed
aggregate gate is not current certification evidence. Restart means a
controlled application-boundary rebuild, not reconnect-only behavior.

Status semantics are intentionally strict:

- `ready` means the current source and named evidence satisfy the row's
  Validator, persistence, restart, and PG18 requirements.
- `gap` means required evidence is missing, failed, unavailable, skipped, or
  not trusted. A gap blocks final V0 certification until resolved or explicitly
  accepted by the final decision owner.
- `intentionally non-Validator-covered` means the contract is a static/core
  or transport-surface contract rather than a public capability scenario, and
  the corresponding core/unit evidence is named.

Every row's historical field is explicitly non-current: the M13 candidate
closure recorded by `docs/tasks/validator-recert/stage-2/t08-coverage-matrix.md#complementary-core--m13-evidence`
(`19c797d`) is historical audit context only and cannot make a row `ready`.
